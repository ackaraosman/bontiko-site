Bontiko is a calm routine companion that lives on a tablet by the door, a watch or a speaker. It walks your child through the steps, then quietly steps back as they get the hang of it. You hear from it only when something slips.

“Okay Bontiko, I’m ready.” Shoes, bag, water bottle. All here. Go have a good day.

How it works

  1. You set the routine once. Morning, after school, bedtime. Steps, times, who does what.
  2. Bontiko runs it. It prompts your child from their own screen or speaker, so the reminding does not come from you.
  3. It fades. As your child completes the steps on their own, the prompts shrink until only one small start cue is left.
  4. You get exceptions only. “Bag not packed, 7:40.” Nothing else, when nothing else is needed.

Why we are building it

Most routine charts, reward apps and timers work for a week and then stop, because the novelty wears off and the job lands back on the parent. Bontiko is built for the opposite outcome: a routine your child owns, and a parent who is no longer the reminder system.

No new hardware. It runs on the tablet you already have in a drawer.
